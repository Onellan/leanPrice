# Visual Direction

Load this for greenfield work, redesigns, major typography/colour/layout decisions, or when a result feels generic despite being technically correct.

## Ground the direction in the subject

Before choosing aesthetics, understand:

- the audience and their environment;
- the surface's primary job and frequency of use;
- what information or artifact matters most;
- the subject's materials, notation, documents, tools, publications, interfaces, and cultural references;
- what would make a polished result feel wrong for this audience.

Distinctiveness should emerge from subject matter and use, not decoration applied after layout.

## Direction contract

For substantial new work, define:

```text
Purpose    audience + primary job + desired outcome
Mode       Persuade | Operate | Read | Experience
Thesis     one specific visual idea
Typography family/roles/scale/density
Colour     surfaces/accent/semantic roles
Layout     composition/grid/alignment/density/responsive strategy
Signature  one memorable visual or interaction idea
```

Use simple wireframes when multiple structures are plausible.

## Generic-design test

Ask whether the same design could serve an unrelated product by replacing only logo and copy. If yes, revise the free design axes.

Challenge defaults such as:

- same-sized rounded cards as the page scaffold;
- decorative gradients, glow, glass, or abstract backgrounds;
- large metric + label + support stats as a default hero;
- all-caps eyebrow labels above headings;
- decorative monospace to signal "technical";
- arbitrary `01 / 02 / 03` numbering;
- identical fade/slide entrances on each section;
- pills everywhere;
- generic near-black/neon or cream/editorial palettes chosen by category rather than brief.

These are not bans when intentionally earned by the brief.

## Spend boldness deliberately

Prefer one strong visual idea with disciplined support. If every section is trying to be memorable, none is.

After the first complete design, identify one decorative idea that can be removed without reducing meaning. Remove it unless it is part of the thesis.

## Typography

Choose type for audience, mode, reading conditions, and identity.

- Operate surfaces often need only one well-tuned family and a tighter scale.
- Persuade/Experience may use a more expressive display voice.
- Read surfaces prioritize sustained reading comfort.
- Keep body measure around 65–75ch where applicable and generally below ~80 characters per line.
- Use clear scale/weight steps.
- Check real copy and long strings at target widths.
- Use tabular numerals where aligned numerical data benefits.

Avoid generated-looking emphasis habits such as arbitrary italic/accent-colour words in headlines, excessive all-caps labels, or metadata typography with no information role.

## Colour

Give colour explicit jobs: surface, foreground, secondary text, action/accent, selected, focus, information, success, warning, destructive/error, border.

Do not invent page-specific colours when semantic tokens already express the need. Do not rely on colour alone for state.

Choose light/dark treatment from use scene, content, brand, and ambient context rather than category stereotypes.

## Layout and rhythm

Structure communicates meaning. Use grouping, alignment, whitespace, borders, headings, and density to encode relationships.

- related items should be closer than unrelated groups;
- headings should visually belong to their following content;
- cards should represent meaningful units rather than default scaffolding;
- operational density can be good when it improves scanning;
- excessive whitespace is not automatically premium;
- avoid accidental nesting and redundant containers.

## Copy as design

Use the user's mental model, not internal implementation language. Use active voice and specific verbs. Controls should name outcomes. Errors should explain the problem and recovery where possible. Keep terminology consistent across the flow.

## Self-critique

Before implementation is considered visually settled, ask:

- Is the primary job obvious?
- Does hierarchy work within seconds?
- Does this belong to this subject?
- Is novelty appropriate to the mode?
- What can be removed?
- Is the direction coherent rather than a collection of effects?
